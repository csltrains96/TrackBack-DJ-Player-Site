PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Overview

TrackBack is a desktop DJ music player for Windows. It's built to work with your local music library on your own machine, and it collects very little data as a result. This page explains what TrackBack does and doesn't do with your information.

What stays on your device

TrackBack scans and reads music files from folders you point it at. Your library, playlists, playback position, ID3 tags, and app settings are stored locally on your machine and are never uploaded to us.

  • Your music files and folder locations
  • ID3 tags and cached album art
  • Playlists, queue, and playback session state
  • App preferences (crossfade duration, gain, theme, etc.)

What does leave your device

A few features require a network request. These only fire when you actively use them:

  • Album art lookup — when you search for cover art, the search term is sent to Discogs and/or the MusicBrainz Cover Art Archive to retrieve matching images.
  • License activation — your license key and a machine identifier are sent to our payment processor, Lemon Squeezy, to validate your purchase and manage activations across machines.
  • App updates — TrackBack checks for new versions periodically. This request includes your current app version only.
Not collected

We don't collect analytics, usage telemetry, listening history, or any data about the contents of your music library. TrackBack doesn't include ads or third-party trackers.

Payment information

Purchases are processed entirely by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. We don't receive or store your card details — only confirmation of a completed purchase and a license key.

Your choices

  • Album art lookup is optional and only runs when you trigger it manually.
  • You can decline automatic update checks in Settings.
  • Uninstalling TrackBack removes all locally stored app data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and note material changes in the app's release notes.
