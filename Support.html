01Common questions

What happens when the 14-day trial ends?

On PC, ChangeCollector locks and stops working until a valid license key is entered. Your data isn't touched — enter a key any time and everything's right where you left it.

I bought the Installed version — can I also use Portable?

Yes. Installed and Portable share the same license, so one purchase covers both PC downloads.

Does my PC license work on Android, or vice versa?

No — PC and Android are separate purchases with separate licenses, since Android is sold and licensed through Google Play.

I got a new PC — can I move my license?

Email support with your details and it'll be sorted out.

Found a bug or something looks wrong?

Send a description of what happened (and a screenshot if you've got one) to the email above — that's the fastest way to get it fixed.
